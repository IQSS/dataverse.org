# Integration asset provenance

Official logo assets are preserved without recoloring. These identify integrations, not sponsors or endorsements. Review vendor trademark permissions before the institutional launch, particularly Dropbox and OSF.

Google Data Commons is an owner-requested roadmap collaboration, not an entry in the linked admin guide. Compute Button is experimental; the Integrations Dashboard is in development. These features have text labels when no distinct logo is verified. RDA and Avgidea use organization marks, not invented product marks.

## Alternatives and suitability

- `GitHub_Invertocat_Black.svg`: standalone current official mark, same ZIP, member `GitHub Logos/SVG/GitHub_Invertocat_Black.svg`. Use with a clear GitHub text label if icon-only.
- `Glyph_128.svg`: Dropbox icon, same ZIP, member `Logos/Dropbox/Glyph/Glyph_128.svg`. Includes prescribed clear space.
- `osf.png`: white OSF wordmark, unsuitable for a white/light background. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CenterForOpenScience/osf.io/develop/website/static/img/osf_logo.png . Preferred dark-letter icon is `osf-icon.png` (165 x 165).
- `rspace-inverted.svg`: white RSpace wordmark unsuitable for a white/light background. Source: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5ffc384cb3a51a7b1c2d57ad/69a1b358c6727818aa792435_rspace_logo_inverted.svg ; linked in https://www.researchspace.com/ . Preferred `rspace.svg` is a colored standalone product symbol.
- `ojs.png` (300 x 195) and `ops.png` (500 x 268) are black transparent wordmarks, suitable for light backgrounds. Their PNG transparency can appear entirely black on a black image viewer backdrop.
- Data Commons is a distinct product mark, not the generic Google logo. Data Commons is not named on the current Dataverse admin integrations page; its inclusion is based on the explicit assignment.

## Brand use notes

- GitHub explicitly permits logos for showing integrations and requires subordinate prominence, preserved design and no implied endorsement: https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo .
- Dropbox prohibits recoloring, distortion and other logo modification; published guidelines ask users to check with Dropbox before commercial/product website use and separately permit functional API uses subject to developer guidelines: https://www.dropbox.com/branding . The asset is from its linked official Brand Partner Toolkit.
- OSF/COS marks are trademarks. COS terms describe a separate permission/license requirement; partner support provides promotional logo resources. See https://github.com/CenterForOpenScience/cos.io/blob/master/TERMS_OF_USE.md and https://osf-partner-support.helpscoutdocs.com/article/619-swag-and-downloadables .
- GitLab points to brand and trademark guidelines: https://design.gitlab.com/ and https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/marketing/brand-and-product-marketing/brand/brand-activation/trademark-guidelines/ .
- PKP staff expressly encouraged use of the OJS logo with a link to the product website: https://forum.pkp.sfu.ca/t/posting-the-ojs-logo-on-the-journal-website/34256 . Do not treat that statement as a separate broad license for every PKP asset.
- No specific reuse license was confirmed for the Data Commons or RSpace marks. Preserve official artwork and use solely to identify the named integrations.
